# Project Specifications & Notebook Ground Truth

## Machine Fleet Specification

- **Models & Fleet Inventory**:
  - **Cobra Moto CX3**: 40 units (Total: 40)
  - **Cobra Moto CX5**: 40 units (Total: 40)
  - **Total Machine Fleet Size**: 80 machines

## Competition Classes & Skill Divisions
- **Age Brackets & Skill Levels**:
  - **Cobra CX3 (Ages 7–9)**:
    - **Class C**: Novice / Beginner skill level
    - **Class B**: Intermediate skill level
  - **Cobra CX5 (Ages 10–12)**:
    - **Class C**: Novice / Developing skill level
    - **Class B**: Intermediate / Advanced skill level
- **Machine Allocation**: 40 CX3 units allocate across CX3-C and CX3-B; 40 CX5 units allocate across CX5-C and CX5-B with rapid battery swap/cooldown rotation in impound.
- **Class C Rider Eligibility**: Proficient use of an electric bicycle qualifies a participant to register and compete in Class C (demonstrating two-wheel balance, throttle control, and braking modulation).
- **Rider Promotion Policy**: Riders who demonstrate proficient motocross skills on track shall be promoted to the higher classification (Class C to Class B) with parental approval, as formally directed by the Track Director.

## Operational Safety & Track Rules
- **Physical Separation Policy**: Machines and humans are separated by default (inactive bikes isolated in technical impound racks/bays).
- **Pre-Staging Dispatch Alert**: Exactly 10 minutes prior to gate staging, pit crew parents are alerted to retrieve their assigned machine from the impound bay and escort it to the staging chute.
- **Pre-Staging Gate Verification**: 6-point safety check (DOT/ECE helmet, chest protector, eye protection, kill switch tether, handlebar padding, and rider state of mind).
- **Session Timing Protocol**:
  - 10-minute on-track riding heat.
  - 3-minute paddock clearing interval.
  - White flag at 1 minute remaining; Checkered flag at 00:00.

## Reservation Window, Payment & Pricing Structure
- **Reservation Window**: Strictly a **60-day reservation window** (bookings accepted from today up to 60 days in advance).
- **Payment Confirmation Protocol**: **7-day payment confirmation prior to the scheduled event** (slots are locked with placeholder zero-down; payment confirmation and charge settlement occur exactly 7 days prior to the session date, or immediately if booking within 7 days).
- **Fee & Membership Pricing**:
  - **Scheduled Block**: **$65** per scheduled block.
  - **Walk-in Entry**: **$85** per block for same-day walk-in riders.
  - **Annual Membership**: **$200/year membership**.
  - **Member Block Rate**: **$40 per block** for active annual members ($25 savings per block).
